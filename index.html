Biography

My name is Adam Adebowale. I am an aspiring software engineer based in Lagos, Nigeria, driven by a deep curiosity about computing systems and a passion for crafting software solutions that address real-world human problems. My journey into tech began with a simple desire to understand what happens under the hood of modern web applications—how text typed into an editor translates into scalable platforms that serve millions.

Throughout my learning journey, I have cultivated strong analytical reasoning, discipline, and attention to detail. As the digital landscape evolves, I am focusing on building a deep foundation in computer science and full-stack development to build resilient, accessible, and user-friendly software products.

Why I Joined the Program

I enrolled in the AltSchool Africa School of Engineering because of its proven track record of equipping African youth with world-class, production-ready engineering skills. Specifically, I joined to:

  • Experience a Structured, Hands-on Curriculum: Move past self-directed guesswork into a rigorous, industry-aligned engineering roadmap.
  • Specialize in AI-Powered Fullstack Engineering: Learn to integrate modern artificial intelligence tools, API integrations, and automation directly into full-stack development workflows.
  • Collaborate with a Community of Builders: Connect, pair program, and brainstorm with peers and mentors across the African continent.
  • Gain Career Readiness: Build practical, portfolio-grade projects and gain the engineering discipline required to stand out in global software engineering teams.

Goals for the School of Engineering

My primary goals for this program include:

  1. Master the Fundamentals: Develop deep fluency in semantic HTML, accessible UI standards, modern JavaScript, backend architecture, and relational/non-relational databases.
  2. Leverage AI Tooling: Harness artificial intelligence to accelerate code generation, testing, debugging, and data modeling.
  3. Ship Production-Ready Applications: Collaborate on team capstone projects that solve tangible problems in African commerce, education, and health.
  4. Launch a Professional Engineering Career: Graduate with distinction, contribute to open-source software, and land a high-impact software engineering role.
